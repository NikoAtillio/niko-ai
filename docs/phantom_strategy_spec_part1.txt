§0  GLOBAL STANDARDS & SHARED DEFINITIONS
All rules in this section apply universally across every strategy detector. No strategy may override these definitions. They form the single source of truth for all shared concepts used in this specification.
§0.1  Candle Anatomy Definitions
All strategies use the following standardized candle component definitions:
Term
Formula
body
abs(close − open)
upper_wick
high − max(open, close)
lower_wick
min(open, close) − low
total_range
high − low
longer_wick
max(upper_wick, lower_wick)
shorter_wick
min(upper_wick, lower_wick)

bearish candle

doji

§0.2  Wick Ratio Formula (Universal)
MANDATORY: This is the single wick ratio formula used across ALL strategies. No alternative formula is permitted.
wick_ratio = longer_wick / body   (where body > 0)
If body == 0 (doji): wick_ratio = longer_wick / total_range
Threshold: wick_ratio ≥ 0.4 is the default minimum for a valid rejection candle.
Rationale: longer_wick / body measures how much the wick extends relative to the committed price movement. A ratio of 0.4 means the wick is at least 40% as long as the body — a meaningful rejection signal.

§0.3  Rejection Candle Standard Definition
Used identically in: TREND_PULLBACK, RANGE_REVERSION, MEAN_REVERT_BAND, MOMENTUM_CONT
BULLISH REJECTION CANDLE — all conditions must be true:
1. lower_wick / total_range >= 0.35   (wick occupies ≥35% of total range)
2. wick_ratio >= 0.4                  (lower_wick / body >= 0.4)
3. close > open                       (bullish close)
4. close >= (low + 0.6 × total_range) (close in upper 40% of candle)
5. body >= 0.05 × total_range         (not a doji)
BEARISH REJECTION CANDLE — all conditions must be true:
1. upper_wick / total_range >= 0.35   (wick occupies ≥35% of total range)
2. wick_ratio >= 0.4                  (upper_wick / body >= 0.4)
3. close < open                       (bearish close)
4. close <= (high - 0.6 × total_range)(close in lower 40% of candle)
5. body >= 0.05 × total_range         (not a doji)
ENGULFING CANDLE (alternative confirmation):
Bullish:  close[0] > open[1] AND open[0] < close[1] AND body[0] > body[1]
Bearish:  close[0] < open[1] AND open[0] > close[1] AND body[0] > body[1]
Note: [0] = current candle, [1] = previous candle

§0.4  Confidence Scoring System
Every strategy outputs a confidence score in [0.0, 1.0]. The scoring system is weighted and rule-tiered.
Rule Tiers:
Tier
Weight
Behaviour
MANDATORY
3 pts
Any single MANDATORY fail → confidence = 0.0 (signal suppressed)
STRONG
2 pts
Failure reduces score significantly but does not suppress signal
OPTIONAL
1 pt
Bonus points — enhances confidence when met

Scoring Formula:
max_score = sum of weights for all rules in strategy
earned_score = sum of weights for all rules that PASS
raw_confidence = earned_score / max_score
confidence = 0.0 if any MANDATORY rule fails else round(raw_confidence, 2)
Example — TREND_PULLBACK with 9 rules:
Rules: trend_confirmed(M), ema_zone(M), rejection_wick(M), adx_filter(S),
       volume_filter(S), fib_depth(S), higher_tf_align(O), session(O), spread(O)

Scenario: trend✓ ema✓ wick✓ adx✓ volume✗ fib✓ htf✓ session✓ spread✗
Earned:   3+3+3+2+0+2+1+1+0 = 15  |  Max: 3+3+3+2+2+2+1+1+1 = 18
Confidence = 15/18 = 0.83

§0.5  Timeframe & Minimum Data Rules
TIMEFRAME SCALING RULE:
All indicator periods are bar-count based, not time-based. A 20-bar EMA means 20 candles regardless of timeframe. A 20-bar EMA on M5 covers 100 minutes. On H1 it covers 20 hours. This is intentional — the strategy logic is calibrated to bar structure, not clock time.
MINIMUM DATA REQUIREMENT (hard rule, non-overridable):
MIN_BARS_REQUIRED = 200
No signal of any kind is generated until 200 bars of history are available.
This ensures: EMA200 is valid, ADX(14) is stable, ATR(14) is reliable,
              BB(20) is valid, pivot detection has sufficient lookback.

§0.6  Regime Hysteresis Rules
To prevent whipsaw re-classification when ADX oscillates near thresholds, asymmetric entry/exit thresholds are used for all regime boundaries.
STRONG TREND:  Enter when ADX > 25  |  Exit when ADX < 22
WEAK TREND:    Enter when ADX 18–25 |  Exit when ADX < 15 or ADX > 27
RANGE:         Enter when ADX < 18  |  Exit when ADX > 21
HIGH_VOL:      Enter when ATR > 2.0×avg_ATR | Exit when ATR < 1.6×avg_ATR
Implementation: Maintain current_regime as state. Only update when exit threshold is crossed, not when entry threshold is merely touched from the other side.

§0.9  Indicator Caching & Computation Order
Performance rule: All indicators are computed ONCE per bar and cached. No strategy detector recomputes an indicator independently.
Per-bar computation order:
  1. Candle anatomy (body, wicks, total_range)
  2. ATR(14)  →  cache["atr"]
  3. EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200)  →  cache["ema20"], etc.
  4. ADX(14)  →  cache["adx"]
  5. BB(20, 2.0)  →  cache["bb_upper"], cache["bb_mid"], cache["bb_lower"]
  6. RSI(14)  →  cache["rsi"]
  7. Volume MA(20)  →  cache["vol_ma"]
  8. REGIME_SWITCH reads cache → outputs current_regime
  9. All strategy detectors read cache + current_regime → output signals
 10. CONFLICT_RESOLVER processes all signals → final output

§0.10  Fibonacci Reference Leg Rule
When any strategy references Fibonacci retracement levels (e.g. 23.6%–78.6% pullback depth), the reference leg is defined as:
Reference leg = the most recently COMPLETED impulse leg in the trend direction.

Definition of "completed impulse leg":
  - A directional move of ≥ 1.5 × ATR(14) from swing low to swing high (bullish)
    or swing high to swing low (bearish)
  - Followed by at least 3 bars of counter-directional or sideways price action
  - The leg endpoint is the extreme price of that move (highest high / lowest low)

If multiple completed legs exist: use the MOST RECENT one only.
If no completed leg exists: Fibonacci filter is SKIPPED (treated as optional pass).

§1  REGIME_SWITCH — Market Classifier
REGIME_SWITCH is not a trading strategy. It is a prerequisite classifier that runs FIRST on every bar before any strategy detector is evaluated. Its output (current_regime) gates which strategies are permitted to generate signals.
§1.1  Classification Logic
Inputs: ADX(14), EMA(20) slope, BB width, ATR ratio

ema_slope    = (EMA20[0] - EMA20[5]) / EMA20[5]   # 5-bar slope %
bb_width     = (BB_upper - BB_lower) / BB_mid
atr_ratio    = ATR[0] / mean(ATR, 20)

Classification (evaluated in priority order):

  if atr_ratio > 2.0:
      regime = HIGH_VOLATILITY   # checked first — overrides all others

  elif ADX > 25 AND abs(ema_slope) > 0.001:
      regime = STRONG_TREND

  elif ADX >= 18:
      regime = WEAK_TREND

  else:   # ADX < 18
      regime = RANGE

  # Apply hysteresis (see §0.6) — only update if exit threshold crossed

§2  TREND_PULLBACK — Strategy Detector
§2.1  Overview
Enters in the direction of the primary trend after a counter-trend pullback to a key EMA zone, confirmed by a rejection candle. Highest-probability setup in trending markets.
§2.2  Regime Gate
Active in: STRONG_TREND (primary), WEAK_TREND (secondary — reduced size)
Inactive in: RANGE, HIGH_VOLATILITY
§2.3  Entry Rules
MANDATORY rules (any fail → confidence = 0.0):
M1. TREND CONFIRMED:
    Bullish: EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 AND close > EMA200
    Bearish: EMA20 < EMA50 < EMA200 AND close < EMA200

M2. EMA ZONE HIT:
    Price has touched or penetrated EMA20 or EMA50 zone
    Zone = EMA ± 0.5 × ATR(14)
    Condition: low <= EMA50 + 0.5×ATR (bull) or high >= EMA50 - 0.5×ATR (bear)

M3. REJECTION CANDLE:
    Bullish rejection candle OR bullish engulfing (see §0.3)
    Bearish rejection candle OR bearish engulfing (see §0.3)
STRONG rules (failure reduces confidence):
S1. ADX FILTER: ADX(14) > 20
S2. VOLUME FILTER: volume > vol_threshold (see §0.7 for threshold by instrument)
S3. FIB DEPTH: pullback depth between 23.6% and 78.6% of reference leg (see §0.10)
OPTIONAL rules (bonus confidence):
O1. HIGHER TF ALIGN: Same trend direction on next higher timeframe EMA alignment
O2. SESSION: Price action during London or NY session (see §0.8)
O3. SPREAD: Current spread < 2 × average spread
§2.4  Confidence Scoring
Rule weights: M1=3, M2=3, M3=3, S1=2, S2=2, S3=2, O1=1, O2=1, O3=1
Max score = 18
confidence = 0.0 if M1 OR M2 OR M3 fails
confidence = earned_score / 18  (if all mandatory pass)
§2.5  Entry & Exit Parameters
Entry:      Next bar open after signal candle closes
Stop Loss:  Below/above signal candle low/high + 0.5 × ATR(14)
Target 1:   Entry + 1.0 × risk (1R)  →  close 50% of position
Target 2:   Entry + 2.0 × risk (2R)  →  close remaining 50%
Stop to BE: Move stop to entry after Target 1 is hit
Time exit:  Close if no Target 1 hit within 20 bars
§2.6  Output Signal Schema
{
  "strategy_id": "TREND_PULLBACK",
  "direction": "long" | "short",
  "entry": float,
  "stop_loss": float,
  "target_1": float,
  "target_2": float,
  "confidence": 0.0–1.0,
  "regime": "STRONG_TREND" | "WEAK_TREND",
  "rules_passed": ["M1","M2","M3","S1","S3","O1","O2"],
  "rules_failed": ["S2","O3"],
  "timeframe": "M5",
  "timestamp": ISO8601
}

§3  BREAKOUT — Strategy Detector
§3.1  Overview
Detects and trades breakouts from consolidation zones, key S/R levels, or volatility compression patterns. Enters on confirmed break with volume.
§3.2  Regime Gate
Active in: STRONG_TREND, WEAK_TREND, RANGE
Inactive in: HIGH_VOLATILITY (too many false breaks in extreme vol)
§3.3  Entry Rules
MANDATORY rules:
M1. STRUCTURE LEVEL IDENTIFIED:
    A swing high/low, S/R zone, or BB band that price has tested ≥ 2 times
    Level tolerance: ± 0.3 × ATR(14)

M2. CLEAN BREAK:
    Close beyond level by ≥ 0.2 × ATR(14) (avoids wicks-only breaks)
    Bullish: close > resistance_level + 0.2×ATR
    Bearish: close < support_level - 0.2×ATR

M3. VOLUME CONFIRMATION:
    volume > vol_threshold (see §0.7) — MANDATORY for BREAKOUT (not optional)
STRONG rules:
S1. RETEST ENTRY (preferred): Price retests broken level from above/below
    Retest zone: level ± 0.5 × ATR(14)
    Retest candle shows rejection (see §0.3) — if no retest, enter on break close
S2. ADX DIRECTION: ADX trending (not flat) in direction of break
S3. NO MAJOR RESISTANCE within 1.5 × risk distance ahead
OPTIONAL rules:
O1. HIGHER TF ALIGNMENT: Break aligns with higher TF trend
O2. SESSION: London or NY session
O3. BB EXPANSION: BB width expanding at time of break
§3.4  Confidence Scoring
Rule weights: M1=3, M2=3, M3=3, S1=2, S2=2, S3=2, O1=1, O2=1, O3=1
Max score = 18
confidence = 0.0 if M1 OR M2 OR M3 fails
§3.5  Entry & Exit Parameters
Entry (break):   Next bar open after break candle closes
Entry (retest):  Next bar open after retest rejection candle closes
Stop Loss:       Below/above the broken level + 0.5 × ATR(14)
Target 1:        1R  →  close 50%
Target 2:        2R  →  close remaining 50%
Stop to BE:      After Target 1 hit
Time exit:       Close if no Target 1 within 15 bars
§3.6  Output Signal Schema
{
  "strategy_id": "BREAKOUT",
  "direction": "long" | "short",
  "entry": float,
  "stop_loss": float,
  "target_1": float,

  "broken_level": float,

§4  RANGE_REVERSION — Strategy Detector
§4.1  Overview
Fades price to the opposite boundary of a defined range. Enters near range extremes with rejection confirmation. Mean-reversion logic within a bounded price structure.
§4.2  Regime Gate
Active in: RANGE (primary), WEAK_TREND (caution — halved position size)
Inactive in: STRONG_TREND, HIGH_VOLATILITY
§4.3  Range Definition
Range is valid when:
  1. ADX < 20 (low directional pressure)
  2. Price has touched upper boundary ≥ 2 times AND lower boundary ≥ 2 times
     within the last 50 bars
  3. Range height ≥ 1.5 × ATR(14)  (meaningful range, not noise)
  4. Range height ≤ 8 × ATR(14)    (not an extended trend disguised as range)

Boundary tolerance: ± 0.3 × ATR(14)
Range midpoint: (upper_boundary + lower_boundary) / 2
§4.4  Entry Rules
MANDATORY rules:
M1. VALID RANGE: Range definition above is satisfied
M2. PRICE AT BOUNDARY:
    Long: low touches lower_boundary ± 0.3×ATR
    Short: high touches upper_boundary ± 0.3×ATR
M3. REJECTION CANDLE: Bullish/bearish rejection at boundary (see §0.3)
STRONG rules:
S1. RSI EXTREME: RSI(14) < 35 (long) or > 65 (short)
S2. VOLUME: volume > vol_threshold at boundary touch
S3. PRIOR BOUNDARY RESPECT: Last touch of this boundary resulted in reversal
OPTIONAL rules:
O1. BB BAND TOUCH: Price touching BB lower (long) or BB upper (short)
O2. SESSION: Active session (not dead hours)
O3. WICK RATIO ≥ 0.6: Stronger rejection (above minimum 0.4 threshold)
§4.5  Confidence Scoring
Rule weights: M1=3, M2=3, M3=3, S1=2, S2=2, S3=2, O1=1, O2=1, O3=1
Max score = 18
confidence = 0.0 if M1 OR M2 OR M3 fails
WEAK_TREND caution: If regime is WEAK_TREND, cap confidence at 0.65 regardless of score.
§4.6  Entry & Exit Parameters
Entry:      Next bar open after rejection candle closes
Stop Loss:  Beyond boundary + 0.5 × ATR(14)
            Long SL: lower_boundary - 0.5×ATR
            Short SL: upper_boundary + 0.5×ATR
Target 1:   Range midpoint  →  close 50%
Target 2:   Opposite boundary - 0.5×ATR  →  close remaining 50%
Stop to BE: After Target 1 hit
Time exit:  Close if no Target 1 within 25 bars
Invalidation: Close beyond boundary by > 1.0×ATR → range broken, exit immediately
