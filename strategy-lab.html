7Structured Strategy Schema

What this does

The structured schema is the machine-readable version of your strategy. It combines the recognised theory, market, timeframes, risk, and execution rules into a payload that the engine can backtest and report on deterministically.
Editing the schema directly is more useful after login or purchase, when you want to fine-tune the code without changing the original strategy notes.
